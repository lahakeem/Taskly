About Taskly

A simple web platform created to help university students manage their academic workload, track deadlines, and organize study schedules.

🎯 Why It Was Created

Taskly was created to solve common challenges like forgotten assignment due dates, overlapping exam schedules, and disorganized study notes across multiple subjects.

Explore Taskly

Click the books, notebook, or lock to explore Taskly.

📖 How It Helps University Students

By keeping all your subjects, homework assignments, lab reports, and exam dates in one organized dashboard, you can prioritize urgent tasks and stay on top of your semester.

Clear deadline tracking ensures you never experience last-minute panic or missed submission windows.

🎓 Main Benefits

Key advantages of using Student Task Organizer:

  • Reduced Stress: Stay informed on upcoming due dates well ahead of time.
  • Clear Priorities: Direct effort toward high-weight midterm exams and lab reports.
  • Better Study Habits: Build disciplined weekly schedules across all enrolled subjects.
  • Simple & Fast: Access your courses and task planner in seconds.
