How It Works

Four simple steps to organize your semester:

  1. Create an account: Register on our Sign Up page with your email.
  2. Add your courses: Enter your enrolled subject codes and details.
  3. Log upcoming tasks: Input assignment deadlines, exams, and lab dates.
  4. Track your progress: Check off tasks as you finish them during the term.
